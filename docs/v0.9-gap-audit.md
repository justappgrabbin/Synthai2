## Critical v0.9 correction

The canonical ordered address is Gate → Line → Color → Tone → Base → Degree → Minute → Second → Axis → Zodiac → House. It must affect candidate constraints and relational construction through explicit source-backed operations with component-level traces. Adding these fields as metadata or embeddings would not meet §68. The document explicitly leaves “12 lines / 12 decimal places,” numeric units and precision unresolved; do not choose a format that pretends to settle them.

The self/other, Evolution and Space requirements describe a user model and a candidate computational dependency. The specification says the order is unresolved. Do not hardcode Space as a center or quietly choose an ordering. Preserve source observations separately from inferred meaning or identity.

Acoustic segmentation, phonetics, phonology, lexical/grammatical tone, music and semantics are distinct levels. Linguistic tone and HD Tone are separate variables. No simulated sensor data should be recorded as observations.
