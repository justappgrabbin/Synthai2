B.A.N.T.U. × 3–2–1

Encoding ↔ six primitive particles ↔ hexagram ↔ neighborhood-driven motion. A runnable bridge experiment, not the complete Synthia swarm.

Each encoded character is one six-position hexagram (3–2–1 groups). For values above 63, an explicit escape sequence of two six-bit hexagrams is used. Particle motion uses an experimental neighbor rule, not an inferred law from the causal-graph document.
